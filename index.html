Sizing Optimization Lab

Replays the enriched trade history under a fixed exit ladder (sell ½ at +1R, sell ½ of the remainder at +2R, trail the final ¼ on the 20-day EMA, hard stop at plannedCut) while sweeping the sizing distance. The cut price stays where it is — only the share count, 1R/2R target locations, and per-trade R denominator change. Allocated dollar risk per trade is held constant.

Caveat: simulator uses daily bars and assumes ideal fills (no slippage, no liquidity ceiling, no cost of capital). Conservative same-day collisions: stop fires before targets. Tight-sizing R-multiples reflect a best-case world.

Iterations are produced by simulate_sizing.py. Run python simulate_sizing.py --iters 100 to append more, or --reset to wipe history. Each row below is one logged run.
